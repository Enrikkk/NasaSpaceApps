## Patterns visible in the winners
- Each winner mapped to one of the 10 award definitions; 3 of 10 came from the LEO commercialization challenge, which was the most represented challenge among winners.
- Every winning page (those read) had: a clear one-paragraph Summary, a demo video/presentation link, a working project link (live site, Vercel/Framer deployment, GitHub Pages, or a Drive folder), a long Project Details section, a candid "Use of AI" disclosure (tools and what humans did vs AI), and itemised NASA Data and Other Data lists naming specific datasets/APIs (e.g. DONKI, NASA POWER, GeneLab OSD-288, Kepler/TESS/JWST, SPARK 2022, DAS).
- Real NASA data was named explicitly and was central to the narrative (live DONKI feed in PureFlow, GeneLab OSD-288 in SpaceGenes+, POWER in Twisters).
- Quality of explanation and traceability counted: winners explained what the data shows, limits and future work.
- Winners came from every kind of local event, including Universal Event (2) and small cities (Aracaju, Arequipa), so no home-field advantage for large venues is visible.
- Ideas ranged from pure software (dashboards, apps) to concept/business designs (AakashNet, Gaia+LEO) and a game; polished prototypes plus a strong story appear in most.
