# Pitch (3 min) and 30-second version

**30 s:** "Field data is scattered. We order it, and a decision algorithm tells you what to plant on your parcel to maximise
its profitability, without breaking the law and with the uncertainty visible."

**3 min:** problem (15 s) -> the parcel seen from space (40 s) -> three rotations with traffic light (50 s) ->
what-if and 2050 (30 s) -> honesty: what we don't know (15 s) -> business and roadmap (15 s) -> close (10 s).

Hook: "This Torre-Pacheco parcel grew melon in 2025. Today it is bare. If it rains like 2 October, its nitrate reaches the Mar Menor.
We see it from space." Keep Occam's razor: one parcel, one comparison, one clear answer.
No NASA logos. English subtitles. Always say which parts are real, simulated or roadmap (see `app/README.md`).
